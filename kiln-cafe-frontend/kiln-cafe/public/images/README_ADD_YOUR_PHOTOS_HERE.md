# 📸 How to Add Your Real Café Photos

## 🎯 Overview
This folder is where you'll place all the real photos from **Abol Garden Cafe** in Soddo.

---

## 📥 Step 1: Download Photos from Google Maps

### Method 1: Direct Download from Google Maps
1. Open your café's Google Maps page: https://maps.app.goo.gl/ba97dYQLdQQ4L6sa6
2. Click on the photos section
3. Right-click on each photo you want
4. Select "Save image as..."
5. Save to this folder (`public/images/`)

### Method 2: Use Your Phone
1. Visit the café location on Google Maps app
2. Take screenshots or save images
3. Transfer to your computer
4. Place in this folder

### Method 3: Take Your Own Photos
Best option! Take high-quality photos of:
- ☕ Coffee ceremony setup
- 🍽️ Food dishes (injera, tibs, kitfo, etc.)
- 🏡 Garden seating area
- 🪴 Outdoor atmosphere
- 👥 Staff and customers (with permission)
- 🎨 Interior décor
- 🌿 Plants and garden details

## 📐 Image Size Recommendations

For best results:
- **Width:** 1200-1600 pixels
- **Height:** 800-1200 pixels
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **File size:** Under 500KB each (compress if needed)

Use free tools to resize/compress:
- [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com/) - Compress images
- [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app/) - Resize and compress
- Windows Photos app - Basic resize

## 🔄 What Happens if You Don't Have Photos Yet?

No problem! The website will use placeholder images temporarily. You can add real photos later:

1. Just place the photos in this folder with the correct names
2. Restart the website (stop and start again)
3. The new photos will appear automatically!
